# Automation Agency Web Designer Playbook

Use this skill when designing, building, and styling high-impact web pages, portals, interactive calculators, and service showcase interfaces for the automation agency.

## Design System & Visual Hierarchy
1. **Color Palette (Enterprise Tech & Innovation)**:
   - Primary Accent: Electric Indigo / Sapphire Blue (`#3B82F6` / `#2563EB`)
   - High-Tech Dark Neutral: Slate Jet (`#0A0F1D` / `#0F172A`)
   - Card Surfaces: Deep Glassmorphism (`rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.7)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`)
   - Energetic Accents: Cyber Emerald (`#10B981`) for live status & ROI, Coral Glow (`#F43F5E`) for calls to action.
2. **Typography**:
   - Modern sans-serif typography (e.g. `Plus Jakarta Sans`, `Inter`, or `Outfit`).
   - Clear visual rhythm with ample whitespace and micro-interactions.
3. **Key Agency Pages & Sections**:
   - **Hero Section**: High-converting hook ("Automatizamos los procesos críticos de tu empresa con SAP BTP, BPM y Chatbots con IA").
   - **Service Matrix**:
     - *SAP Integration Suite*: Cloud Integration (CPI), S/4HANA OData, BAPIs, ERP sync.
     - *BPM & Orchestration*: Camunda, Process Automation, approval flows, zero manual bottleneck.
     - *Smart Chatbots*: WhatsApp Business, 24/7 omni-channel customer service, CRM lead qualification.
   - **Interactive ROI Calculator**: Allows SMBs and enterprise clients to estimate hours and budget saved.
   - **Live Interactive Demo Playground**: Embedded mock chatbot and live process flow visualization.
   - **High-Trust Social Proof & Case Studies**: Testimonials, SLA metrics (99.9% uptime, 4x faster operations).
   - **Lead Capture & Booking Form**: Direct appointment scheduling.
